Prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a polished wide isometric restaurant cutaway illustration for Ekatra POS website's interactive guided workflow. Entire business shown at once on one continuous isometric floor, clean editorial vector-like 3D/isometric illustration, restrained detail, warm off-white background #F8FAFC, neutral gray furniture, subtle indigo #5048E5 and emerald #10B981 accents. Wide landscape composition about 3:2 with generous margins around the whole floor. Five clearly separated physical stations, positions essential for overlay UI: 1. front-left at approximately (22% width, 65% height), a cashier counter with touchscreen POS, receipt printer, cashier and one customer. 2. back-left at (28%, 32%), open kitchen with chef, stovetop, prep table and cooking pot. 3. back-center at (57%, 19%), pantry shelving with food ingredients, rice sacks, vegetables and a refrigerator. 4. front-right at (73%, 67%), meal pickup/service counter with prepared plates and staff handing a meal to a customer. 5. back-right at (81%, 34%), a small owner's office desk with laptop displaying an abstract small bar chart and one seated owner. A few simple dining tables in center connect the establishment. Approximately six people total, small and natural. Unified orthographic isometric perspective, precise furniture, clear station silhouettes. Visual hierarchy must leave each station readable when scaled to 700px wide. No text, no numbers, no labels, no logo, no speech bubbles, no floating UI cards, no arrows or connectors: these will be added interactively in HTML. No large foreground objects, no dark background, no fake statistics. Keep every station fully in frame and visually separate. White space between stations to fit future overlay callouts. Professional friendly small restaurant environment, original design rather than copying another brand.
